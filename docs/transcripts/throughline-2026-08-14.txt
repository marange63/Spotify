Turning to the applied frontier. One thread worth naming before we close, because it's now running a second straight day. Yesterday this show flagged that nearly every briefing was quietly running the same check — set the marketed number against the harder one that's supposed to back it up. Today that test didn't fade, it sharpened. Databricks' valuation step-up shrank once you traced its actual timeline. Private equity's GP-led secondaries hit a record with no disclosed pricing-to-NAV to validate it. AgiBot's shipment lead undercut the volume story Unitree's IPO was priced on. And DeepSeek's own published table showed the eye-catching twelvefold number applied narrowly, while the figure that actually matters to most developers moved a fraction as much. Two days running now, the pattern hasn't been that these headline numbers are false — it's that the harder number, when it exists at all, keeps coming in smaller than the claim built on top of it.
