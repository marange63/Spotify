Today's spine is the repricing and what it has not reached yet. A rate path moved twenty-four points on tone. Gold, the dollar and bitcoin obeyed within hours. Credit, private credit and the oil chokepoint did not. Four of these six reads are about whether that gap closes.

Now the thing that did not move, which is the more interesting trade. Going into the speech, high-yield spreads sat near 281 basis points, against a long-run median near 450. I'd put that at about two-in-five — under even, because rich spreads are sticky and stay rich for a long time. It is not lower because this show defended that tightness on two premises, falling long yields and a dead energy impulse, and one just broke. To bet for the call, buy puts on the high-yield fund, ticker HYG. To fade it, own that same fund.
