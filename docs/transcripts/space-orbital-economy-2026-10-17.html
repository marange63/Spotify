Now the operational reality underneath that ambition. New Glenn's recent record involves two separate incidents, and it's worth keeping them apart. The first came in April. A New Glenn mission failed in flight and did not place AST SpaceMobile's broadband satellite into the correct orbit. Filing now is rational if Blue Origin expects to fly in 2027. The bear case is about capital. This is money going into cadence infrastructure before the rocket has shown it can deliver that cadence.
