Now the applied frontier, where the same forward-financing question shows up in hardware.

Robotics and Physical AI has Figure AI finally disclosing output, all of it company claims: its Helix model succeeded on about 56 percent of household chores in homes it had never seen, against 9 percent without pretraining. Figure's compute commitment to Nscale starts at 3.5 billion dollars against cash just above 2 billion, but that capacity isn't due to deploy until late 2027, so it's a financing question for then, not now.

The desk's read is that design is heading toward free and the money is moving downstream, as Warp Sciences, led by OpenAI's former product chief Kevin Weil, raised 215 million dollars to build the physical synthesis and testing capacity that models can't replace.
