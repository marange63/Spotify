From rates to lending, Private Credit and the Corporate Credit Cycle finds the week's one-way migration of risk from banks to private lenders picking up a counterweight. The desk's inference is that private lenders keep what banks don't want, a mix shift that loss numbers won't show until something breaks.

But just three megadeals made up nearly a third of that, and the direction of the exit count depends on whose data you use. The desk calls it a top-heavy recovery, not a reopened exit window for the median portfolio company.
