That is the worst possible price to set policy against. A rate hike defends inflation expectations. It cannot add a barrel of supply. Tightening into a supply shock is a credibility operation, not a demand operation, and markets price those two things very differently. That pressures dollar-denominated flows into emerging markets and into speculative assets generally, independent of what happens to US equity multiples.
