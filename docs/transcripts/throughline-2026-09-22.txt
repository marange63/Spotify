Start with the macro backdrop. Strategic Power has the long-term uranium price at a nominal record, ninety-six dollars a pound, and the world's largest producer is refusing to chase it. Digital Money picks up the same gap between a headline and its mechanism. Bitcoin printed an eight-month high on Monday, but roughly seven of every eight dollars of liquidations came from shorts, so forced covering did the work rather than fresh demand. Spot ETF flows were essentially flat in the window before the move, and the stablecoin base underneath all of it has not grown in three months, which makes this the most fragile price of the cycle rather than a confirmation of one.

Now into the AI and compute stack, where the question all day is what has been built versus what has been announced. Semiconductors and Compute Infrastructure finally has a number for the packaging bottleneck, because supply-chain checks put TSMC's advanced-packaging capacity roughly doubling by the end of 2028, well before Monday's groundbreaking in Kaohsiung opens a site that does not run until late 2029.
