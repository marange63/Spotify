Good morning, this is The Throughline for Thursday, August 20th, 2026. Today's headline: one Treasury decision moved almost everything. Wednesday's announcement that Treasury will at least double its long-end buyback operations, from two billion to at least four billion dollars per operation starting September 9th, is the driving event in two separate briefings today — it cooled a 19-year-high 30-year yield, lifted equities, and triggered an eight-percent bitcoin short squeeze within hours. A single fiscal-management decision, not a Fed move, repriced bonds, stocks, and crypto in one session — and it lands as the sharpest expression yet of a fiscal-credibility story that's separately been pulling the dollar down and real assets up for weeks.

Start with the macro backdrop. Worth watching whether that's a one-day event or the start of fiscal policy, not marketing spin, becoming the thing that moves every market at once.
