The desk's so-what is that one company's governance risk has become index risk, while credit and the uranium miners are still de-risking underneath the bounce.

With futures leverage at its lowest since early March, the desk expects spot selling rather than forced liquidations to set the next move.

So the desk's open risk isn't today's default print, but a performing book still carried at model marks.
